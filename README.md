Open index.html in a browser. The reader, chapter search, and mathematics work offline. Course recordings and external references require internet access. The chapter print view opens the worked solutions. Editable manuscripts are in chapters/ and standalone lab scripts are in code/.

The recording links navigate to related passages. Original examples, derivations, figures, and exercises develop the subject beyond those passages. The source pass used chronological caption checks with closer reading at selected topic transitions. English automatic captions may misrecognize notation. The lecture inventory records track digests and timing. The course recordings remain with their original publisher. [Lecture inventory](lecture-inventory.csv)
